Welcome to Karnataka

Karnataka is a beautiful state in southern India known for its rich history, magnificent temples, waterfalls, wildlife, beaches and delicious food. Bengaluru is the capital city of Karnataka.

🏞️ Tourist Places

Mysuru

Famous for Mysuru Palace, Chamundi Hills and Dasara celebrations.

Coorg

Known for coffee plantations, hills, forests and scenic landscapes.

Gokarna

A coastal destination famous for beaches and beautiful sunsets.

Hampi

A UNESCO World Heritage Site filled with spectacular ruins and temples.

🏛️ Historical Places

Hampi

Historic ruins of the Vijayanagara Empire with temples and monuments.

Belur

Famous for the magnificent Chennakeshava Temple.

Badami

Known for ancient rock-cut cave temples and sandstone cliffs.

Pattadakal

A historic temple complex showcasing remarkable Indian architecture.

💦 Waterfalls

Jog Falls

One of India's spectacular waterfalls, located in Shivamogga district.

Abbey Falls

A beautiful waterfall surrounded by the greenery of Coorg.

Shivanasamudra Falls

A scenic waterfall on the Kaveri River.

Gokak Falls

A scenic waterfall known for its natural surroundings and bridge.

🍛 Famous Foods of Karnataka

Masala Dosa

A crispy dosa served with flavorful potato masala and chutney.

Bisi Bele Bath

A traditional spicy rice and lentil dish.

Ragi Mudde

A nutritious traditional dish made from finger millet flour.

Mysuru Pak

A popular traditional sweet from Mysuru.

✈️ Travel Information

✈️ By Air: Karnataka has airports including Bengaluru, Mangaluru, Hubballi and Mysuru.

🚆 By Train: Major cities and tourist destinations are connected by railway networks.

🚌 By Bus: Government and private buses connect cities and tourist locations.

🚗 By Road: Karnataka has highways connecting major cities and neighboring states.

🌦️ Best Time: October to February is generally comfortable for sightseeing in many parts of Karnataka.
